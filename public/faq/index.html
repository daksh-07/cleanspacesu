A few quick answers before you get a tailored quote.

How much does a clean cost?

Clean Space does not list fixed public prices on this site. Use the quote flow to share the kind of clean, home size and timing, then confirm the tailored scope directly with Clean Space.

What if I do not know which clean I need?

Choose “Not sure yet” in the quote flow. The goal is to start the conversation without forcing you to diagnose the job yourself.

What areas can I ask Clean Space to focus on?

The current service information covers regular home care, one-off resets, kitchen and bathroom details, living spaces, floors, dusting and finishing touches.

Can I ask about weekly or fortnightly cleaning?

Yes. The quote flow lets you indicate weekly, fortnightly, one-off or flexible frequency so the conversation starts with the rhythm you have in mind.

How do I choose a time?

You can indicate as soon as possible, this week, next week or flexible timing. Final availability is confirmed directly with Clean Space.

How do I contact Clean Space?

You can call or text 0426 379 247, use the quote flow, or message @cleanspaceau on Instagram.

Where is Clean Space based?

The current service information is for Melbourne, Victoria.
